My story

I started with numbers. I stayed for people.

Before I ever read a chart, I studied mathematics. I did my Master's in Maths (M.Sc) and trained as a teacher (B.Ed) at the University of Jammu. Numbers taught me to look for patterns and to trust what I could check.

That is exactly what drew me to astrology, numerology and palmistry. To me they are pattern languages. The more I studied them, the more clearly they described the people in front of me: their timing, their strengths and the same story they kept living again and again.

But I noticed something that changed how I work. A reading could show someone why they kept repeating the same fear, relationship or money pattern, and still the pattern stayed. Knowing wasn't enough. An expensive remedy can't rewrite a belief your mind learned as a child.

So I went looking for tools that actually change the inner program. I trained in mind reprogramming and became a certified trainer. I trained in hypnotherapy, became a Reiki Master, and learned Lama Fera healing, which I now teach. Today I bring them together: your chart, numbers and palm show the timing and the pattern, simple rituals and upay support you, and mind reprogramming helps you change it.

About three years ago I opened Ashima Empowers in Surrey, British Columbia. I also started sharing short, honest videos about overthinking, relationships and self-worth. A community of more than 24,000 people has grown around them, and one reel has been watched over 2.2 million times. Every week I hear from people who finally feel understood.

My promise is simple: I will never use fear to keep you coming back. I want you to leave every session clearer, calmer and more in charge of your own life.Ashima
