FAQs

Read before you book.

Which session should I start with?

One clear question: the 10-minute One-Question Consultation ($27). The full picture of career, relationships, timing and the patterns holding you back: the 1-Hour Private Consultation ($199). Ongoing change: apply for coaching or join the VIP Circle.

How can astrology help with anxiety or depression?

Your chart shows the pattern and the timing. The change comes from the NLP work: finding the belief or fear underneath and replacing it. This supports your wellbeing but is not medical or psychological treatment. If you have a clinical concern, please also speak with a licensed professional.

What details do I need?

Your full name, date of birth, time of birth (as exact as you know) and place of birth. For online palm reading, clear photos of both palms.

Online or in person?

Both. Video sessions worldwide, or in person at 5550 152 St #201, Surrey, BC (Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm Pacific).

Will you sell me gemstones or remedies?

No. No gemstones, no paid rituals, and never fear to make you buy anything.

Can I cancel the VIP Circle or weekly group?

Yes, anytime before your next billing date.
