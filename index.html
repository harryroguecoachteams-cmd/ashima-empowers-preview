FAQs

Read before you book.

Which session should I start with?

One question on your mind: the 10-minute One-Question Consultation ($27). A few questions: the 30-Minute Private Consultation ($99). The full picture of career, relationships, health, timing and the patterns holding you back: the 1-Hour Private Consultation ($149). Ready for lasting change: apply for 1:1 coaching or join the VIP Circle ($199/month). Compare all sessions.

How can astrology help with anxiety or depression?

Your chart, numbers and palm show the pattern and the timing. The change comes from the mind reprogramming work: finding the belief or fear underneath and rewiring it, supported by simple rituals and upay. This supports your wellbeing but is not medical or psychological treatment. If you have a clinical concern, please also speak with a licensed professional.

What happens after I pay?

You'll get an email and a short details form: your full name, date, time and place of birth, and clear photos of both palms and your fingertips. Then Ashima's team contacts you on WhatsApp to set your time.

Online or in person?

Both. Video sessions worldwide, or in person at 5550 152 St #201, Surrey, BC (Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm Pacific).

Do you sell gemstones or expensive remedies?

No. Ashima gives simple rituals and upay you can do yourself, never fear and never costly remedies.

Can I get a refund?

All payments are non-refundable. You can reschedule a session with at least 24 hours' notice, and cancel a monthly membership (VIP Circle or Empower Your Inner World) anytime before the next billing date. See the refund policy.
