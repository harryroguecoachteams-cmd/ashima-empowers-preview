VIP Circle FAQ.

When does my membership start?

Straight after payment. You'll get an email with your welcome, your course and community access, and the private WhatsApp community invite. Ashima's team then books your first call.

How do the two monthly calls work?

Two private video calls with Ashima every month, booked at times that suit you. Unused calls don't roll over to the next month.

How do I cancel?

Anytime, in two clicks, from Manage my membership. You keep access until the end of the month you've paid for, and you keep lifetime access to the course.

Can I get a refund?

All payments are non-refundable, including monthly membership payments. Cancel before your next billing date to stop future payments. See the refund policy.

Is this medical or psychological treatment?

No. Readings and mind reprogramming are guidance for personal growth. They can sit alongside your doctor or therapist, never in place of them. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Canada & US) or your local emergency number.
