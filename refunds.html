Refunds & cancellations

Simple and clear: every payment is non-refundable, and here is how rescheduling and cancelling work.

Privacy policyTerms of serviceRefunds & cancellations
Last updated 25 November 2026

All payments are non-refundable. This covers every consultation, coaching payment and membership payment, including the One-Question Consultation, the 30-minute and 1-hour consultations, 1:1 coaching, the VIP Circle ($199/month) and Empower Your Inner World ($21/month). Please read the details of a session before you book.

Consultations

One-Question Consultation ($27), 30-Minute Private Consultation ($99) and 1-Hour Private Consultation ($149).

  • Rescheduling: free, once, with at least 24 hours' notice. Message Ashima's team on WhatsApp or email us.
  • Less than 24 hours' notice, or a missed session: the session is treated as used. In a genuine emergency, message us and we'll do our best to help.
  • Book within 60 days: unbooked sessions expire 60 days after payment.

1:1 coaching

Your plan and its price are agreed with you before you pay. Coaching payments are non-refundable. Sessions can be moved with at least 24 hours' notice.

Memberships

The VIP Circle ($199/month) and Empower Your Inner World ($21/month).

  • Cancel anytime from Manage my membership. Enter the email you paid with and Stripe sends you a secure link.
  • Cancelling stops all future payments. You keep access until the end of the month you've already paid for.
  • Monthly payments, including the first one, are non-refundable, and unused calls don't roll over.

If Ashima has to cancel

If Ashima needs to cancel or can't deliver a session, you choose a new time that suits you, or a full refund for that session.

Questions

Email support@ashimaempowers.com or message Ashima on WhatsApp with your name, the email you paid with and what you bought.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under the consumer protection laws where you live.
