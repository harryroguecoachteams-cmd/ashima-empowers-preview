Refunds & cancellations

Clear and fair: what happens if you need to reschedule, cancel or ask for a refund.

Privacy policyTerms of serviceRefunds & cancellations
Last updated 24 November 2026

We want you to feel completely comfortable booking with Ashima. Here is exactly how rescheduling, cancellations and refunds work for each service. All prices are in US dollars.

Consultations

One-Question Consultation ($27) and 1-Hour Private Consultation ($199).

  • Not booked yet: if you change your mind before choosing a time, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of payment.
  • Rescheduling: free with at least 24 hours' notice.
  • Cancelling with 24 hours' notice or more: full refund, or move the session to a new time.
  • Less than 24 hours' notice, or a missed session: not refundable. In an emergency, message us and we'll do our best to help.
  • After the session: completed sessions are not refundable, because Ashima's time and preparation have been given.

Coaching discovery calls

The 10-minute ($10) and 20-minute ($20) discovery calls follow the same 24-hour rule: full refund or a new time with at least 24 hours' notice, no refund after that or once the call has taken place.

Memberships

The VIP Circle ($299/month) and Empower Your Inner World ($29/month).

  • Cancel anytime from Manage my membership. Enter the email you paid with and Stripe sends you a secure link.
  • Cancelling stops all future payments. You keep access until the end of the month you've already paid for.
  • We don't give refunds for partly used months.
  • Forgot to cancel? If you're charged for a new month and haven't used anything from it yet (no call booked, no new content opened), contact us within 7 days of the charge and we'll refund that month.

If Ashima has to cancel

If Ashima needs to cancel or can't deliver a session, you choose: a new time that suits you, or a full refund.

How to ask for a refund

Email support@ashimaempowers.com or message Ashima on WhatsApp with your name, the email you paid with and what you bought. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe. Your bank usually shows them within 5 to 10 business days.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under the consumer protection laws where you live.
