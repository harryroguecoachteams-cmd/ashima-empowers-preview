01 · Logo

The lotus and the guiding star.

A fine-line lotus (growth from difficult ground) under a four-point star (the chart, and the light that guides). Drawn in one gold line so it feels calm, not loud. The name is set in Cormorant Garamond with wide spacing; "EMPOWERS" sits underneath in small Inter capitals.

Horizontal: website header, email signature

Profile seal (Google, Instagram, YouTube) and mark alone (favicon, stamps)

Use it like this

  • Gold mark + ivory name on plum, or gold-deep mark + plum name on ivory.
  • Keep clear space around it equal to the height of the star.
  • Smallest size: mark 24 px tall on screen, 10 mm in print.
  • On photos, only over a calm dark area, never over a face or pattern.

Never

  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows or glows.
  • Recolour it (no purple, blue, pink or rainbow gradients).
  • Swap the font or set "Ashima" in script.
  • Put the old sunburst or "AE" monogram next to it. Retire them.

02 · Colour

Three colours, used with restraint.

Deep plum and ivory are the grounds (plum replaced black in v4: warmer, and at home in astrology). Gold is the only accent: buttons, the key phrase in a heading, lines and icons. No black, navy, pink or bright blue.

06 · Imagery

Warm light, gold-foil art.

Photos: Ashima in natural warm light, real rooms, brass and marigold details, never cold blue studio light. Illustrations: always the gold-foil style below (plum to gold to ivory), so they sit with the photos.

Voice

  • Calm, honest, warm. Speak to one person.
  • Name the feeling (anxiety, low days), then the way through.
  • "No fear, no gemstone selling" is a promise. Keep it everywhere.

Avoid

  • Zodiac emoji, neon, purple galaxies, crystal-ball clichés.
  • Fear words: "doshas will ruin", "urgent remedy".
  • More than one gold button side by side.
