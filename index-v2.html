Where should I start?

If the same pattern has followed you for years, apply for 1:1 coaching. It takes about 3 minutes, and you hear your plan before you decide anything. If you'd like Ashima with you every month, join the VIP Circle ($199/month). For one session, the 1-Hour Private Consultation ($149) gives you the full picture, the 30-Minute ($99) covers a few questions, and the 10-minute One-Question Consultation ($27) answers the one that keeps you up. To start small, join Empower Your Inner World ($21/month) or the free WhatsApp community. All prices are in USD. Compare everything.

How can astrology help with anxiety or depression?

Your chart, numbers and palm show the pattern and its timing, and seeing it laid out can be a relief in itself. The change comes from the mind reprogramming work: finding the fear or belief underneath and rewiring it, with simple rituals and upay alongside. This supports your wellbeing. It isn't medical or psychological treatment, so if you see a doctor or therapist, please keep going, and if you have a clinical concern, speak with a licensed professional.

What happens after I pay?

You'll see a short details form straight after paying (also sent by email): your full name, your date, time and place of birth, and clear photos of both palms and your fingertips. Then Ashima's team messages you on WhatsApp to set a time that suits you.

Both. On video from anywhere in the world, or in person at 5550 152 St #201, Surrey, BC, Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm Pacific.
