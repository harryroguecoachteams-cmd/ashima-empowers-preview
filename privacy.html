Privacy policy

How Ashima Empowers collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Privacy policyTerms of serviceRefunds & cancellations
Last updated 25 November 2026

Ashima Empowers ("we", "us") is run by Ashima Gautam in Surrey, British Columbia, Canada. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, email address and phone or WhatsApp number, when you sign up for the free Calm Mind Reset, apply for coaching, book a session or message us.
  • Reading details: your date, time and place of birth, and photos of your palms and fingertips if you choose to send them. We only use these to prepare and give your reading.
  • Coaching application answers: what you'd like help with and anything you choose to share about your situation.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. We see your name, email, the amount and the payment status. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Email activity: whether our emails are opened or links are clicked, so we know what is helpful.

How we use it

  • To deliver the sessions, coaching, membership and free resources you ask for.
  • To book, confirm and follow up on your sessions.
  • To send the Calm Mind Reset and a few follow-up emails with guidance and offers. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email.
  • To manage payments, memberships and our accounting records.
  • To answer your questions and keep our services safe.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we never share your reading or coaching details with anyone else.

We use trusted providers to run the business. They only process your information to provide their service to us.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Stripe, Brevo, YouTube and Instagram may set their own cookies when you use their forms, checkout pages or videos.

How long we keep it

We keep client records for as long as you work with us and for up to 7 years afterwards for tax and accounting reasons. Email sign-up details are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

Your choices and rights

  • Ask to see the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Withdraw your consent to emails at any time, or ask us to delete your information where we are not required to keep it.

To do any of these, contact us below. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

Keeping it safe

We use reputable providers with strong security, limit who can see your information, and only ask for what we need.

Age

Our services are for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
