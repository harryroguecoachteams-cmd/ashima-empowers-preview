Why is the discovery call paid?

It keeps Ashima's calendar for people who are serious about change, and you get genuine guidance on the call instead of a sales pitch.

What happens after the call?

If coaching is a good fit, Ashima explains your personal plan, how often you'll meet and the investment. There is no pressure to say yes on the call.

Yes, by video from anywhere in the world, or in person at 5550 152 St #201, Surrey, BC.
