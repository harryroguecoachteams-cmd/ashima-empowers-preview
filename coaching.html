Questions

Coaching FAQ.

Is there a discovery call?

No. Fill in the application and Ashima's team reviews it personally. If coaching is a good fit, they get back to you within 24–48 hours to schedule a call.

How much does coaching cost?

Every plan is customised, so the investment depends on what you need and how long. You'll hear it on your call, with no pressure to say yes. Payments are non-refundable.

Is coaching online?

Yes, by video from anywhere in the world, or in person at 5550 152 St #201, Surrey, BC.

What if coaching isn't the right fit?

Ashima's team will tell you honestly and point you to what suits you best: a private consultation, the VIP Circle, Empower Your Inner World, or a licensed professional.

Is this medical or psychological treatment?

No. Readings and mind reprogramming are guidance for personal growth. They support your wellbeing but don't replace a doctor or licensed therapist. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Canada & US) or your local emergency number.
