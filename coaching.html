Questions

Before you apply

What happens after I apply?

Ashima's team reads your application personally. They get back to you within 24 to 48 hours to set up a call, where you hear the plan made for you and what it costs.

How much does coaching cost?

Every plan is written for one person, so the cost depends on what you need and for how long. You'll hear it on your call, with no pressure to say yes. All payments are non-refundable, so use that call to ask everything before you decide.

Is coaching online?

Yes, by video from anywhere in the world. Or in person at 5550 152 St #201, Surrey, BC.

What if coaching isn't right for me?

Ashima's team will tell you honestly and point you to what suits you better: a private consultation, the VIP Circle, Empower Your Inner World, or a licensed professional.

Is this medical or psychological treatment?

No. Readings and mind reprogramming are guidance for personal growth. They can sit alongside your doctor or therapist, never in place of them. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Canada & US) or your local emergency number.
