Before you press record

  • Phone sideways, back camera, on a tripod at eye level.
  • Frame yourself from the chest up, a little space above your head.
  • Face a window. No window or lamp behind you.
  • Quiet room. Fan and AC off, phone on silent.
  • A small clip-on mic makes the biggest difference to quality.
  • Wear a solid jewel colour: plum, emerald, royal blue or maroon.
  • Look into the camera lens, not at the screen.
  • Record one part at a time, 2–3 takes each.
  • Wait 2 seconds before you start and after you finish each take.
  • Mistake? Just pause and start that line again. We edit it out.

When you're done
