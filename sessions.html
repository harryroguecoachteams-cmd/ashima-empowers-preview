Questions

Questions about booking

What happens after I pay?

You'll see a short details form (also sent by email): your name, date, time and place of birth, and photos of both palms and your fingertips. Then Ashima's team messages you on WhatsApp to set your time.

Online or in person?

Both. On video from anywhere in the world, or in person at 5550 152 St #201, Surrey, BC, Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm Pacific.

Will I be told to buy gemstones or an expensive puja?

No. Ashima gives simple rituals and upay you can do yourself at home. And she has made a promise: she will never use fear to keep you coming back.

Can I get a refund?

All payments are non-refundable. You can move your session once to a new time with at least 24 hours' notice. See the refund policy.

Is this medical or psychological treatment?

No. Readings and mind reprogramming are guidance for personal growth. They can sit alongside your doctor or therapist, never in place of them. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Canada & US) or your local emergency number.

Still unsure?

If you're torn, apply. Ashima's team will point you to the right fit.
