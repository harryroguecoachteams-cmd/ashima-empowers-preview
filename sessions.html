Questions

Booking FAQ.

What happens after I pay?

You'll see a short details form (also sent by email): your name, date, time and place of birth, and photos of both palms and your fingertips. Then Ashima's team messages you on WhatsApp to set your time.

Online or in person?

Both. Video sessions worldwide, or in person at 5550 152 St #201, Surrey, BC (Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm Pacific).

Do you sell gemstones or expensive remedies?

No. Ashima gives simple rituals and upay you can do yourself, never fear and never costly remedies.

Can I get a refund?

All payments are non-refundable. You can move your session to a new time with at least 24 hours' notice. See the refund policy.

Is this medical or psychological treatment?

No. Readings and mind reprogramming are guidance for personal growth. They support your wellbeing but don't replace a doctor or licensed therapist. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Canada & US) or your local emergency number.
